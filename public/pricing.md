# Pricing — GlowUP.

Last updated: 2026-10-02
Source of truth: https://glowupbeautysolutions.com/#pricing
Site: https://glowupbeautysolutions.com

GlowUP. is a salon operating system. Glo AI is included in every paid plan. Packaging is hybrid (base subscription + included Glo conversations), not pure per-seat.

## Solo

- Price: $39/month (billed monthly)
- Target: 1 stylist / booth / suite / independent
- Included AI: 150 Glo conversations / month
- Trial: 14 days
- Features:
  - 1 live calendar + online booking
  - Client CRM (notes and visit history)
  - Glo AI front desk (chat)
  - Deposits path
  - Leads capture
- Scale: add chairs, then upgrade to Floor (no re-platform)

## Floor

- Price: $149/month (billed monthly)
- Target: multi-stylist, one location (most salons)
- Included AI: 1,000 Glo conversations / month
- Pilot: 30 days on the house, then the card. This is the plan we sell.
- Features:
  - Multi-stylist live calendar spine
  - Full CRM (formulas, preferences, history)
  - Glo AI books into real chairs 24/7
  - Team tools and owner approvals
  - Deposits and SMS reminders
  - Ops multi-device sync

## Brand

- Price: $299+/location/month or custom quote
- Target: 2+ locations
- Included AI: 5,000 Glo conversations / month
- Trial: talk to us — aaron.jawsai@gmail.com
- Features:
  - Everything in Floor
  - Multi-location admin
  - Advanced reporting / analytics
  - Priority support
  - Custom AI routing by location

## Notes

- AI overage soft-caps at 120% of included; upgrade plan or wait for next month.
- Voice telephony may use a separate allowance. Voice is not live yet (chat Glo is beta).
- Annual discount available on request (~15%).
- Compare: one OS vs booking tool + a separate AI receptionist ($49–200+/mo).
