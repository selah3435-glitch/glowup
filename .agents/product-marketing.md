# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-08-24

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** GlowUP. is the AI-powered salon operating system — Glo books the live multi-stylist calendar after hours, then the same OS runs CRM, deposits, and growth as the floor adds chairs.
**What it does:** Multi-tenant salon OS for booking, client CRM, deposits, and Glow Agents (Fill the Book, Chair Content, Reputation, Front Desk). Flagship is **Glo**, the AI receptionist that books into the real book — not a voicemail, not a separate AI widget bolted onto Vagaro.
**Product category:** Salon software / salon operating system (owners search “salon booking software,” “salon CRM,” “AI receptionist for salons”).
**Product type:** B2B SaaS (open beta). Hybrid AI: base subscription + included Glo conversations + overage.
**Business model:** Solo $39/mo (150 Glo chats) · Floor $149/mo (1,000) · Brand $299+/location (custom). 14-day trial on Solo/Floor. Annual ~15% on request. Not per-seat.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Independent stylists / booth-suite renters; 2–20+ chair floors; multi-location beauty brands. GTM may start with fast solo wins; brand always says we grow with the floor.
**Decision-makers:** Salon owner (buyer). Manager (champion). Stylist (user). Front desk (user).
**Primary use case:** Stop losing after-hours and missed-call bookings; run one book across chairs instead of DMs + paper + a separate AI bot.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Book the chair when the phone is down (Glo 24/7).
- See the whole floor on one calendar without double-books.
- Rebook and win-back from the live book (Fill the Book), not a marketing tool that doesn’t know who’s empty.
**Use cases:** After-hours holds onto the live book; deposits on high-ticket color; one OS when hiring chair two; multi-location admin later without a re-platform.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Operational friction — phones, DMs, paper, split booking/SMS/CRM — so inquiry volume never becomes a filled chair, especially after hours.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Vagaro / Fresha / Gloss Genius / StyleSeat: booking + marketplace gravity; AI front desk is an add-on or missing.
- Separate AI receptionist ($49–200+/mo): not native to the live multi-stylist calendar.
- Pen-and-paper / DM-to-book: cheap until it leaks every after-hours inquiry.
**What it costs them:** Empty chairs after 6pm, no-shows without deposits, owners answering IG DMs during color. **Do not invent occupancy %, ticket $, or conversion rates.**
**Emotional tension:** “I’m running a floor on my phone.” Fear of switching the book. Anxiety about AI posting guest faces.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Gloss Genius, Mangomint, Vagaro, Fresha, StyleSeat, Booksy, Boulevard — same job (run the salon). Fall short for GlowUP. buyers when AI is bolted on and growth tools don’t sit on the live book.
**Secondary:** Square + Mailchimp + a chatbot; Millennium / desktop salon software — split stack or legacy.
**Indirect:** Personal assistant / front-desk hire; Instagram DMs as the book.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Glo books the **live multi-stylist calendar** (native, not a widget).
- Continuous OS: Solo → Floor → Brand without a forced re-platform.
- CRM-native growth (Fill the Book, Chair Content) from appointments + consent — not a Hootsuite clone.
- Human-in-the-loop social: stylist drafts → owner approves. No guest faces without consent.
**How we do it differently:** One book. AI included. Agents that know who’s empty tomorrow.
**Why that’s better:** One invoice vs booking software + AI receptionist. Floor language, not “solo app you’ll outgrow.”
**Why customers choose us:** After-hours books land on the real chair. We grow with the floor.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| “We’re already on Vagaro / Gloss Genius.” | Keep the consumer marketplace if you want; the leak is after-hours and the split stack. Glo books *your* chairs. |
| “AI will mess up the book / post client faces.” | Human approve on content. Consent locks. Glo holds onto real availability — not a voicemail. |
| “We’ll switch when it’s more mature.” | Open beta: Glo chat live; voice coming. Phase-honest. Don’t fake 2,000 salons. |
| “$149 is a lot.” | vs booking + separate AI $49–200. Floor is the salon plan; Solo is $39. |

**Anti-persona:** Guests looking to book a haircut (wrong side of the marketplace). Nail-only chains that need POS/inventory depth we don’t ship yet. Owners who want fully autonomous posting with no approval. Anyone asking us to invent occupancy or discount % as proof.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Missed calls, DM-to-book, double books, paying twice (booking + AI bot), outgrowing a solo app.
**Pull:** Glo on the live book; one OS as chairs are added; Fill the Book from real gaps.
**Habit:** The current book is the business. Staff trained on Vagaro. Guest booking links already printed.
**Anxiety:** Migration, lost history, AI mistakes, looking cheap/spammy on social.
