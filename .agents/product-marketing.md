## Product Overview
**One-liner:** GlowUP. is the salon OS for multi-stylist floors — Glo books the live calendar after 7pm so empty chairs take deposits, then the same desk runs CRM and the book.
**What it does:** Multi-tenant salon OS for native desk, live multi-stylist book, client CRM, and deposits. Flagship is **Glo**, the AI receptionist that books into the real book — not a voicemail, not a widget bolted onto Vagaro. Glow Agents (Fill the Book, Chair Content, Reputation, Front Desk) sit on that book.
**Product category:** Salon software / salon operating system (owners search “salon booking software,” “salon CRM,” “AI receptionist for salons”).
**Product type:** B2B SaaS (open beta). Hybrid AI: Floor subscription + included Glo conversations + overage.
**Business model:** GTM is **Floor only: $149/mo, 30 days on the house for the pilot — not free forever.** Product catalog still lists Solo $39 / Floor $149 / Brand $299+/location; Solo is a SKU, not the hunt. Annual ~15% on request. Not per-seat. Website setup/brand/migration is a **hedge only for paying GlowUP. customers** — not a general web shop; “I also build any website” is a different team. Do not add POS, payroll, or a marketplace. Voice only when chat Glo is not dropping bookings. **$0 new marketing spend.**

## Target Audience
**ICP:** Owners of **multi-stylist floors** (2+ chairs), **same city or same niche**, who feel **empty-chair pain after 7pm**. They already have a book (often Vagaro). They are not booth renters.
**Target companies:** Independent multi-stylist salons and floors in one city cluster or one niche cluster. Not solo/booth-suite renters already on GlossGenius or Booksy.
**Decision-makers:** Salon owner (buyer). Manager (champion). Stylist (user). Front desk (user).
**Primary use case:** Stop losing after-hours inquiries into empty chairs; book deposits onto the live multi-stylist calendar from a native desk.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Book the chair when the phone is down (Glo 24/7 on the live book).
- See the whole floor on one calendar without double-books.
- Take deposits on the same book the inquiry landed on.
- Switch without killing the current book (concierge: CSV + Zoom; Vagaro parallel 14 days).
**Use cases:** After-hours holds onto the live book; deposits on high-ticket color; one native desk across chairs. Not POS. Not payroll. Not a consumer marketplace.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** After-hours empty chairs — inquiry volume never becomes a booked deposit because the phone, DMs, and paper are down when the floor is closed.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Vagaro / Fresha / Gloss Genius / StyleSeat: booking + marketplace gravity; AI front desk is an add-on or missing; switch cost is the lock-in.
- Separate AI receptionist ($49–200+/mo): not native to the live multi-stylist calendar.
- Pen-and-paper / DM-to-book: cheap until every after-hours inquiry leaks.
**What it costs them:** Empty chairs after 7pm, no-shows without deposits, owners answering IG DMs during color. **Do not invent occupancy %, prices, ticket $, conversion rates, IG strategy, or salon counts.**
**Emotional tension:** “I’m running a floor on my phone.” Anxiety is **switch cost** — losing the current book — not “is AI cool.”

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Gloss Genius, Mangomint, Vagaro, Fresha, StyleSeat, Booksy, Boulevard — same job (run the salon). Fall short for GlowUP. buyers when AI is bolted on and growth tools don’t sit on the live book.
**Secondary:** Square + Mailchimp + a chatbot; Millennium / desktop salon software — split stack or legacy.
**Indirect:** Personal assistant / front-desk hire; Instagram DMs as the book.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Glo books the **live multi-stylist calendar** from a **native desk** (not a widget).
- Deposits on the same book the after-hours inquiry hit.
- Concierge migration: CSV + Zoom; current book (Vagaro) parallel 14 days — switch cost is handled, not ignored.
- CRM-native growth (Fill the Book, Chair Content) from appointments + consent — not a Hootsuite clone.
- Human-in-the-loop social: stylist drafts → owner approves. No guest faces without consent.
**How we do it differently:** One live book. Glo included. Agents that know who’s empty tomorrow. We do **not** win by adding POS, payroll, or a marketplace.
**Why that’s better:** Native desk vs booking software + separate AI receptionist. Floor language. Migration is a concierge, not a CSV in a help center.
**Why customers choose us:** After-hours inquiries become booked deposits on the real chair. We grow with the floor we already sold — we do not hunt booth renters.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** After-hours empty chairs, missed calls, DM-to-book, no deposits on the inquiry that came in at 9pm.
**Pull:** Glo on the live multi-stylist book; deposits on that book; native desk; concierge that does not strand the current book.
**Habit:** The current book *is* the business. Staff trained on Vagaro. Guest booking links already printed.
**Anxiety:** **Switch cost.** The opponent is the cost of leaving the current book — lost history, a dead week, staff who only know Vagaro — not a feature checklist. Answer: concierge CSV + Zoom; run the current book in parallel 14 days.
